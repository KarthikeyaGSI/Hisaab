Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: desktop opening screen for Hisaab, an Indian public development funding atlas; static visual concept of a buildable interactive Three.js relief map. Generate a very high quality wide 16:10 composition.
Primary request: The first visit shows only a magnificent full 3D map of India, not a data dashboard. Make the landscape itself the entire experience. An elegant, quietly breathtaking dimensional relief atlas, physical terrain, contemporary browser rendering, realistic landscape textures with a restrained art-directed finish.
Subject and geography: One continuous recognisable north-up mainland India outline occupying about 75 percent of the frame width and height, seen from an elevated slightly oblique camera from the south, with the country's proportions retained, Kashmir at upper left, Gujarat on the western flank, the long southward peninsula, eastern Assam corridor and the full Northeast clearly included. Include correctly positioned small Andaman and Nicobar islands to the southeast and Lakshadweep to the southwest. No neighbouring countries visible. Terrain must follow Indian regional geography: detailed snow-tipped mountain ridges in the far north, lush forested Northeast hills, golden sandstone and sand of the Thar desert in the northwest, pale green fertile north-central plains, subtle branching river systems, muted green and ochre Deccan plateau, a narrow verdant Western Ghats escarpment along the west of the peninsula, coastal lowlands. Mountains have gently exaggerated relief, still a map, no gigantic fantasy mountain spikes, no exaggerated tall cutaway slab sides. Fine actual-looking valleys, shaded ridges, fields as a subtle surface texture, dark forest canopy as texture, quiet blue river lines, turquoise coastal shallows fading into deep desaturated teal water. Keep rivers plausible, not giant random waterfalls.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed softly lit mineral-blue sea along the coasts blending into a light atmospheric warm-grey background outside the map at northern land boundaries. An isolated country relief for visual focus, not a literal island. No visible global map, no continent, no planet, no pedestal, no objects outside India.
Lighting and material: warm early morning light from upper left, clean cool fill, soft terrain self-shadowing, subtle ambient occlusion, realistic rock and snow normals, gentle atmospheric depth without obscuring terrain. No harsh neon, no space imagery, no heavy fog. A rich landscape against a calm light background, detailed yet serene. No depth-of-field blur; whole map is legible.
UI: Hisaab brand in small dark navy system sans-serif reading exactly "hisaab." in the upper-left. A compact rounded softly frosted pill centered near the bottom reading exactly "Explore India". A tiny lower-right circular compass/reset icon. Those three UI elements only; no title, no slogan, no paragraphs, no header bar, no financial year, no numbers, no financial cards, no statistics, no location dots or pins, no legend, no state/city labels, no state borders, no screenshot frame, no browser chrome. The map should be unobstructed. Polished Apple-inspired typography, quietly rounded controls. Mockup must look like a finished website screenshot rather than a poster or infographic.
Constraints: Show the full country including Northeast and islands, never crop India. Avoid invented buildings, monument collage, people, political colour boundaries, fluorescent outlines, cheap low-poly triangular look or video-game UI. Do not add data. This is the clean initial state.
