# Draft Goa RTI: monthly scheme-wise expenditure returns

**Status:** prepared, not filed. The owner must confirm the current PIO entry in the Goa RTI portal and provide applicant details and any filing fee.

**Suggested authority:** Directorate of Planning, Statistics & Evaluation, Government of Goa. The Directorate's official page identifies the office's PIO and says the Directorate compiles state socio-economic data. A Goa order says departments are to submit major-head-, scheme-, and minor-head-wise monthly expenditure under Revenue and Capital heads, including Central and State schemes, to the Directorate.

**Period:** FY 2025–26 and FY 2026–27 through the latest completed reporting month held by the authority.

## Request text

Please provide electronic copies of the following existing records held by the Directorate of Planning, Statistics & Evaluation concerning monthly government expenditure reporting for FY 2025–26 and FY 2026–27 through the latest completed month available:

1. The current order, circular, reporting format, and instructions requiring departments to submit major-head-wise, scheme-wise, and minor-head-wise monthly expenditure returns, including any later amendments.
2. The compiled monthly expenditure returns or consolidated reports received by the Directorate for the stated period, in their existing electronic form where held. Please include the reporting month/cutoff and the department and scheme/head labels shown in the source.
3. Existing field definitions or instructions that distinguish budget estimate, revised estimate, allotment/authorization, release, expenditure booked, cumulative expenditure, recoveries, surrender, and Central/State share.
4. Existing registers showing which departments submitted or did not submit each month's return, and any recorded missing-period notices or follow-up letters.
5. Existing reference tables that link the expenditure returns to treasury/account codes, scheme identifiers, district, work/project identifiers, bill or voucher references, where those fields are already maintained.
6. If the Directorate does not hold any item above, please identify the public authority or office recorded as its custodian, if known, and provide the relevant record-transfer or routing details held by the Directorate.

Please supply the records as maintained and do not create a new analysis. If a file is large, please provide an official download link or allow inspection/copying in the prescribed manner. Redact bank-account numbers and other sensitive personal identifiers while retaining public business/vendor names and official transaction references where disclosure is permitted.

Filing this request, supplying personal details, and paying any fee are owner actions. The order establishes a reporting requirement, not proof that complete monthly returns exist or are public.
