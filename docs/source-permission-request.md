Subject: Permission to reproduce attributed Union Budget expenditure data in Hisaab

I am developing Hisaab, a public-interest platform that helps people understand development spending in India through original government records.

I request permission to extract, normalize and display data from the Union Budget expenditure statements, including ministry/demand totals and relevant scheme provisions, for the financial years available on the official India Budget website. The requested use includes automated retrieval of publicly available PDF/XLSX files through a reasonable, documented schedule, storage of originals for verification, and republication of attributed figures with links to the authoritative documents.

We will distinguish budget estimates, revised estimates, reported actuals, transfers, project sanctions, contracts and payment evidence. Department-wide figures will not be presented as individual contractor payments. We will preserve financial years, units, source dates and document references, and show coverage limitations. The PDF will remain authoritative if it differs from another format.

Please confirm whether this use is permitted under the website copyright and hyperlinking policies, the attribution language you require, any limits on automated retrieval, and whether an official bulk dataset or documented API is available. Please also clarify whether access permission and republication permission differ for historical records.

If this request should go to another office, please direct me to the appropriate contact.
