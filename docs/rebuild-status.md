## Review the app

1. Open http://127.0.0.1:3000/ for the clean India opening. Choose a state or open the exploration controls.
2. For a populated funding example, choose **FY 2024–25**, **Released** and **Assam**. The reviewed received-funding subset is ₹745.02 crore. Development within the area remains unavailable until matching evidence is reviewed; the programme/source panels explain the distinction.
3. Open Map layers and independently select reviewed projects, historical locations and state funding. Click a historical dot to inspect its original identity and location; it has no implied payment link.
4. Inspect source, role, observation and tracking panels. Private `/admin/intake` and `/admin/complaints` require the owner authenticator session.
5. Use `docs/data-acquisition-contract.md` and the 36 prepared state/UT jobs for the separate collection work. Collectors return private originals and proposed joins; a job being prepared does not mean it has run or its evidence has been approved.
