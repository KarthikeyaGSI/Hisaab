# Nationwide development spending scope

User clarification, 6 October 2026: Hisaab should follow development spending throughout India, including railway stations, toilets, roads and hospitals. Road schemes are an initial acquisition source, not the product boundary.

## Product target

Cover Union ministries, state/UT departments, district bodies, municipalities, panchayats and relevant public implementing agencies. Include new construction, upgrades, repairs, maintenance and supporting goods/services where official records establish a development purpose. Retain original spending classifications; do not assume that all development expenditure is capital expenditure.

Offer both a national financial view and a geographically searchable project view. National or programme-wide spending may have no single project location. Never force an unlocated expenditure onto a map point.

For each supported record, distinguish budget estimate, revised estimate, allocation/release, administrative sanction, procurement, award, invoice/payment, refund/reversal, utilisation, reported completion and independent evidence. Each fact needs its source, financial year, units, date, locator and available identity. Absence of a stage means unavailable evidence, not zero spending.

## Financial reconciliation

Represent relationships between funding sources, grants, implementing agencies, projects, contracts and payment events. A Union release passed through a state and paid to a contractor is the same funding chain; those amounts must not be added together as three separate expenditures. Show transfers separately from expenditure and commitments separately from cash paid. Retain amendments, shared funding, multi-year allocations, refunds and cumulative reports explicitly. Exact decimal arithmetic and source identities remain mandatory.

Reconcile programme/project sums against official budget and accounting totals only where years, jurisdictions, classifications and accounting bases are comparable. Publish any residual as unexplained or unmatched coverage; never manufacture individual works to balance totals.

## Acquisition priorities

1. Union budget/expenditure statements and state Finance/Appropriation Accounts establish financial context and coverage baselines.
2. Continue existing PMGSY and JJM acquisition; add housing, sanitation, health, education, railways, highways and urban development through verified official routes.
3. Connect source-specific procurement, treasury/payment, municipal and panchayat records where accessible and permitted.
4. Obtain missing invoices, measurement books, utilisation certificates and completion records through authorized exports or user-controlled requests.

Source-route discovery, public accessibility and reproduction permission are distinct checks. Publicly visible information does not establish a complete public bulk payment ledger. No credentials, CAPTCHA bypass or private beneficiary/payment details are needed to invent coverage.

## Public interface and acceptance

Users should navigate India → state/UT → district → locality → work, or ministry/scheme → allocation → implementation → contract/payment. Each work has an evidence timeline. A source/sector/year coverage view shows acquired, reviewed, published and missing records, update dates and unresolved matches. National totals must explain exactly which records they include.

The ambition is each rupee of development spending. The current implementation cannot certify exhaustive nationwide transaction coverage. Completeness must be demonstrated per source, year, jurisdiction and stage, with documented gaps and reconciliations. Current private candidate counts are not published or independently verified spending.
